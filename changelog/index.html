    v1.0.0 - 2026-11-12¶

    • Vectorize the crystal graph construction by @jcwang587 in #163
    • Pool crystals with scatter_reduce by @jcwang587 in #164
    • Load checkpoints with weights_only=True and make the bundled models predict in physical units by @jcwang587 in #165
    • Batch inference in cgcnn_pred by @jcwang587 in #166
    • Remove the semi-metal classification checkpoint by @jcwang587 in #170
    • Add a Matbench export script for retraining the models by @jcwang587 in #171
    • Unify the dataset classes and the training loops, and remove classification by @jcwang587 in #173
    • Fix the MSE scaling in metrics_text by @jcwang587 in #174
    • Support Python 3.15 by @jcwang587 in #175
    • Show the NaCl structure in the docs with bravais by @jcwang587 in #176
    • Train on standardized targets and keep the checkpoint with the best validation MAE by @jcwang587 in #177
    • Bundle models retrained on Matbench, with their test errors by @jcwang587 in #178
    • Return the pooled crystal features as descriptors by @jcwang587 in #179
    • Raise an error when the training targets are missing or constant by @jcwang587 in #180
